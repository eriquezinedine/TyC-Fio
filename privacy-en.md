# Fio — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 17, 2026_

This policy explains what data Fio collects, what it uses it for, and what you can do about it. It is written to be understood, not to hide anything.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Fio ("we") is the controller of the data we collect through the mobile app and this website.

For any privacy question you can write to **mndmandalorian@gmail.com**.

## 2. What data we collect

Account data: name, email address and your hashed password, or the identifier Apple or Google gives us if you sign in with them.

Physical profile data: age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal. We use them to calculate your calories and macros.

Nutrition data: the meals you log, the foods and ingredients, the amounts, and the photos you upload to the scanner.

Training data: routines, exercises, sets, reps, weight lifted, session duration and history.

Third-party health data: if you allow it, steps and activity from Apple Health. We only read what you permit and you can revoke it from your phone's settings.

Technical data: device model, operating system, app version, language and crash reports.

We do not collect precise location and we do not access your contacts.

## 3. What we use your data for

To make the app work: saving your meals and routines, computing your calorie balance and syncing across your devices.

To analyze the photos you upload to the scanner and identify the foods in them.

To keep your account secure and prevent abuse.

To improve the app using aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

To reply when you write to support.

We do not use your health or nutrition data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

## 4. Legal basis

We process your data to perform the contract you accept when creating an account (providing the service), for legitimate interest (security and product improvement), and with your consent where health data or Apple Health access is involved.

You can withdraw your consent at any time; that does not affect processing carried out before.

## 5. Scanner photos

When you scan a plate, the photo is sent to our servers to be analyzed and to estimate the foods and their calories.

The photo stays attached to that meal in your history so you can review it. It is deleted when you delete the meal.

We do not use your photos to identify you, nor to train models shared with third parties without your permission.

## 6. Who we share it with

Infrastructure providers hosting the database and files, under contract and only to provide the service.

Image analysis providers for the meal scanner.

Crash reporting and usage analytics services, with aggregated or pseudonymized data.

Authorities, when a law or court order requires it.

We never sell your data or hand it to advertisers.

## 7. International transfers

Some providers are outside your country. When that happens we rely on standard contractual clauses or other recognized mechanisms to protect your data.

## 8. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is active.

If you delete your account, we erase your personal data within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep.

Crash logs are kept for a maximum of 90 days.

## 9. Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, object to it and request a portable copy.

The fastest way is from the app, under Profile → Settings. You can also write to **mndmandalorian@gmail.com**.

If you think we haven't handled it properly, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country.

## 10. Minors

Fio is not directed at children under 16. If we find an account belonging to a minor without parental authorization, we delete it.

## 11. Security

We encrypt traffic in transit, store passwords using secure key derivation functions and limit internal access to data.

No system is infallible: if a breach affected you, we would tell you.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change something relevant, we update the date above and notify you inside the app before it takes effect.

## 13. Complaints and account deletion

If you have any complaint, claim or question about Fio, write to **mndmandalorian@gmail.com**. We reply within 15 business days.

To **delete your account** and all your data you have two options:

1. **From the app:** Profile → Settings → Delete account. The account closes right away.

2. **By email:** write to **mndmandalorian@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete account". We'll ask you to confirm and process it within 30 days.

Deleting your account erases your profile, your meals and photos, your routines and training history, and your measurements and goals. We only keep anonymized records that no longer identify you and whatever the law requires us to retain.
